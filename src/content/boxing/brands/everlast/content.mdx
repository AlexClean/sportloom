<h2 className="article-h2">Everlast's History and Place in Boxing</h2>
<p className="article-p-mt2">Everlast began in New York in 1910, founded by Jacob Golomb. Its origins were in swimwear, before its relationship with Jack Dempsey helped establish its boxing identity. Everlast's own history records Dempsey winning the world heavyweight championship in 1919 wearing gloves made by Golomb. That gives the brand a genuine connection to boxing's past, rather than a recently invented heritage story.</p>
<p className="article-p-mt4">Today, the useful way to understand Everlast is as a broad boxing equipment brand. Its identity includes gloves, footwear, bags, and coaching equipment, with products serving different budgets and training situations. Someone attending fitness classes and someone preparing for competition can both encounter Everlast without shopping for comparable gear.</p>
<p className="article-p-mt4">American origins also do not mean every product is made in the United States. Check the country-of-origin label for the exact item if manufacturing location matters to you. A brand's founding location cannot answer that question for every glove or shoe it sells.</p>
